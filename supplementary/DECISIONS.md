# Design decision log

Each entry records what was decided, what else was considered and the evidence behind the choice. Section
numbers refer to the report.

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Evidence / reason | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Implement the optimised model (Model B); keep the direct translation (Model A) only as the experimental baseline. | Implement Model A. | Section 4: Model B needs 23% fewer logical accesses on the six-query workload with identical results. | `import.txt` builds Model B; `supplementary/import_baseline.txt` builds Model A. |
| D2 | **O1** – overlapping PERSON roles and disjoint SUBJECT types become labels on one node. | Subtype nodes joined by `IS_A`. | Priya Raman is student, staff and guardian at once; removing O1 costs +45% on Q1. | `MATCH (p:Staff&Guardian)`; the `subject_kind` discriminator is retired; validations 8.5–8.6 check totality and disjointness. |
| D3 | **O2** – SUBJECT_ENROLMENT and its 1:1 RESULT folded into one `TOOK` relationship with six properties. | Two nodes and three relationships. | Largest single effect: removing it costs +16% on the workload and +24% on Q4. | Results are read from the relationship; the `TOOK.status` and `TOOK.competency_outcome` indexes support it. |
| D4 | **O3** – PERSON_PHONE pivoted into `phone_mobile`, `phone_home`, `phone_work`. | `PhoneNumber` nodes; a list property. | The data hold at most one number per type; cost-neutral (−0.4%) but 27 fewer nodes and simpler queries. A list would lose the phone type. | A fourth phone type would need a schema change (accepted trade-off). |
| D5 | **O4** – invoice totals, payments and balance materialised on `Invoice`. | Summing lines and payments in every query. | Removing it costs +48% on Q3, the finance query. | Import section 7.2 must be re-run after new payments (stated as a limitation). |
| D6 | **O5** – explicit `NEXT_TERM` chain. | Sorting terms by date in each query. | Small saving at four terms (+0.6% without it), growing with history; Q2 reaches "next term" in one hop. | Derived in import section 7.1. |
| D7 | Billing-account category enforced by copying `account_no` onto `HOLDS_ACCOUNT` and declaring it unique. | Application-level check only. | Assessment 2 feedback; one declarative constraint replaces three UNIQUE indexes and six triggers in SQLite. | Needs Neo4j 5.7+; validation 8.4 confirms each account has exactly one allowed holder; 8.8 demonstrates the rejection. |
| D8 | Ternary COURSE_OFFERING, aggregations ENROLMENT and PLACEMENT and weak entity INVOICE_LINE reified as nodes with composite uniqueness constraints. | Hyperedges approximated by several relationships. | A relationship joins exactly two nodes; `LEADS`, `TOOK` and `GOVERNED_BY` need a node to attach to. | Composite constraints `offering_key_unique`, `enrolment_key_unique`, `placement_key_unique`, `invoice_line_key_unique`. |
| D9 | Rejected: invoice lines as a list property; a materialised grade point average. | – | A list breaks the weak entity's identity and line-level queries; results change every term and no use case needed a GPA. | Recorded in Section 2.3. |
| D10 | Fixed reporting date 2026-09-14 in every query. | `date()` (today). | Results must be reproducible for marking and for the expected-results file. | Change one literal to run the queries on another date. |
| D11 | Evaluate centrality against independent ground truth (efficiency loss, students taught) instead of choosing by intuition. | Pick PageRank by reputation. | Path-based measures agree with network damage (ρ = 0.90–0.93); degree-type measures do not (0.29–0.49). | Recommend betweenness, with harmonic as the cross-check and degree for workload (Section 6.4). |
| D12 | Recommend a hybrid for placement matching: program filter, then KNN (Euclidean) on the performance profile. | Similarity alone; the program rule alone. | Similarity alone did not beat random ranking; within a program all variants matched the rule on hits and KNN-Euclidean is deterministic and rarely tied. | PRG103 has no placed peer (cold start): a content rule is needed (Section 7.4). |
| D13 | Stay within Community Edition features. | Enterprise existence and node-key constraints. | Neo4j Desktop's default and the unit's environment. | NOT NULL and cardinality rules are checked by validation 8.7. |
